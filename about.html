About MushHarvest

Growing Quality From the Ground Up

Who We Are

Fresh Mushrooms, Thoughtfully Grown

MushHarvest is focused on cultivating high-quality button mushrooms in a clean, hygienic and carefully controlled environment.

We believe that good produce begins with good growing conditions. That is why every stage, from compost preparation to harvesting, is monitored with care.

Our aim is to deliver mushrooms that are fresh, uniform, nutritious and suitable for homes, restaurants and businesses.
